Built around the way you already work

Every part of the workspace is real and usable on day one — no locked features, no upgrade wall.

Bring your own model

Add OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a local Ollama model in Settings → Providers. Keys are typed once, kept in your browser, and sent only to that provider.

Projects

Group related chats under a project. Open one and your sidebar, new chats and memories all switch to that context.

Memory

Write down the facts you're tired of repeating. Switchable per project, and everything you leave on goes into the prompt of every real model call.

Skills

Save the prompts you use all the time. One click sends them, or drops them in the message box so you can fill in the blanks first.

Files & voice

Attach images and files to a message, click any image to see it full size, and dictate with the mic instead of typing.

Sign in, switch, lock

Google sign-in with your photo next to your messages, a switch-account picker, and an optional PIN that locks the app on this device.

Where your data lives

Chats, projects, memories, skills, preferences and provider keys are stored in this browser only — there is no Notal server holding your history. Clearing your browser data clears the app.

The built-in notal generic model gives written-out example replies so you can see the interface work. Real answers start once you add a provider key, and then your messages go directly to that provider.
