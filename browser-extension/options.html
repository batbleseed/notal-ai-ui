Which pages may ask

This companion answers a page that says it is Notal, and no other. Any page on localhost or 127.0.0.1 counts as one without being listed — that is how the app is usually run, and a local server is one this machine started. Add an address below for anything else, such as a Notal you host on .github.io.

A page opened straight off disk is not trusted, and cannot be: any downloaded file would otherwise be able to drive your other tabs. Serve the app over http://localhost… instead.

    What this companion can do to your other tabs

    Listing tabs, reading the text of a page, and mapping its links, buttons and fields happen on their own, because they only look. Everything that changes something — opening a tab, moving one, clicking, typing, closing — arrives in Notal as a card that says what it intends, and nothing happens until that card is approved.

    Typing refuses a password field, and refuses a field whose label has changed since the page was mapped. When this companion is working in a tab you are not looking at, that tab shows a mark in its title area while the work lasts.

    Nothing is sent anywhere: this companion talks to the tabs in this Chrome and to the Notal page that asked, and it has no server of its own.
