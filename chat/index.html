          What stays, and what does not

          Refresh or close the tab
          Your chats, projects, memory, skills, models and settings all come back. They are saved on this device as you go.
          A different browser, phone or incognito window
          Starts completely empty. There is no server holding a copy to pull from, so nothing carries across.
          Signing in with Google
          Brings in your name and profile picture for the greeting and the avatar. It does not upload or sync your chats, your memory or your API keys.
          Clearing this site's browsing data
          Deletes all of it for good. Export what you want to keep before you do.
